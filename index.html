About the platform

01

What is CEC?

Community Energy Compass (CEC) is an interactive tool for exploring different ways to meet energy goals. It brings together power system planning results and information about local impacts so communities can compare options across cost, land use, jobs, emissions, and other priorities. Rather than presenting one preferred plan, CEC helps users see the choices and trade-offs available to them.

02

Methodological footing

CEC starts with a power system model that identifies a least-cost plan under a defined set of assumptions and constraints. Modeling to Generate Alternatives (MGA) then searches for other plans that meet those constraints at similar cost. Where supported, Modeling to Generate Continuous Alternatives (MGCA) lets users explore combinations of those plans. The resulting portfolios provide a structured way to examine trade-offs, rather than treating any one modeled plan as a prediction.
